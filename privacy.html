PRIVACY POLICY
Tidywick
Effective: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what Tidywick does with information. It is written for a general audience, including parents.

1. Who we are
Tidywick is made by Yuval Gal.
Email: yuvalgal017@gmail.com
Postal address and telephone: available by email at yuvalgal017@gmail.com.

2. What we collect
We do not collect personal information. The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, photos, contacts, or payment details. It does not create an account. It does not use advertising, analytics, crash reporters, or tracking. We run no servers. We do not use an advertising ID.

The game can vibrate the phone when haptics are on. You can turn haptics off in Settings. That does not collect information. Playing a level does not use the network.

Optional gem purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. You pay Google, not us, and your card or payment details go only to Google. The game receives only a confirmation that a pack was bought (the pack name and a purchase code) so it can add your gems. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details from these purchases. The price you are charged is the price Google Play shows on the buy button, in your local currency.

3. What stays on your device
To remember your game, Tidywick saves a small file only on this device. That file can include:
- level progress, stars, coins, gems, and lives
- which renovation tasks you have finished
- sound, music, and haptics on or off
- that you agreed to these terms
- purchase codes of gem packs, so the same purchase is never counted twice

This file never leaves your device. We cannot see it. Apple, Google, and other companies are not given it by us.

4. Children
Tidywick is a casual puzzle for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children (including children under 13, or under 16 in some countries). Because we do not collect personal information, we do not need a parent’s email or a birth date to play. If you are a parent and think a child sent us personal information by email, write to yuvalgal017@gmail.com and we will delete it.

5. Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share player data. There are no advertising networks, analytics tools, or tracking kits in the game. The only third-party kit is the Google Play Billing library, used only when you choose to buy gems.

If you contact us by email, we will use that email only to answer you.

6. How long data is kept, and how to delete it
On-device save data stays until you erase it or uninstall the game.
- In the game: open Settings, tap Erase saved data, then tap again to confirm. That deletes progress, coins, gems, lives, renovation, and purchase codes stored on this device.
- Or delete the app. That removes the save file from this device.

Erasing or uninstalling does not delete Google Play’s record of a charge, and it does not refund you. Refunds, if any, are requested from Google Play.

There is nothing for us to delete on our side, because we do not store your game on a server. There is no account to close.

7. Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. For Tidywick, the only data we hold is an email you choose to send us. We do not hold a player profile. To use those rights, email yuvalgal017@gmail.com.

8. Apple and Google
Apple and Google have their own rules for the App Store and Google Play. Their privacy policies apply to the stores and to payments you make through them (Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy), not to this game’s save file.

9. Changes
If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the text in the app. If we ever start collecting personal data (for example accounts or ads), we will update this policy first and ask for any consent the law requires.

10. Contact
Questions about privacy: yuvalgal017@gmail.com
